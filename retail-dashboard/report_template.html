Key findings (all markets)

Data & method. 1.07M invoice lines, Dec 2009 – Dec 2011. Removed: exact duplicate rows (the source sheets overlap), cancellations and bad-debt adjustments, non-product lines (postage, fees, manual adjustments, gift cards), zero/negative quantities and prices, and the partial month Dec 2011. Revenue = quantity × unit price (GBP). Customer metrics use lines with a customer ID. Cohorts start Jan 2010 — customers first seen in Dec 2009 may have bought before the data begins.

Source. Chen, D. (2012). Online Retail II [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D · licensed under CC BY 4.0.
