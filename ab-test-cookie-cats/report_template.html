Cookie Cats — Moving the First Gate

Retention

Share of players who came back 1 and 7 days after install

Sample Ratio

Players per group: under 30 rounds (never reached level 30) vs 30+ rounds (could reach it)

Who Saw the Gate

Share of players by game rounds played in the first 14 days

Effect by Segment

Group B − Group A, 7-day retention, percentage points with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals

Conclusions

Recommendations

Method. Retention differences: bootstrap (10,000 resamples) 95% CIs, two-proportion z-test p-values, Bayesian probability that Group B is better (Beta(1,1) prior). Game rounds: winsorized at the pooled 99th percentile (one player logged ~50k rounds) and Mann-Whitney test. Sample ratio: χ² test against a 50/50 split. Composition-adjusted estimate: within-segment differences weighted by the pooled segment shares. Power: two-proportion normal approximation, α = 0.05, 80% power.

Data. "Mobile Games: A/B Testing" (Cookie Cats), public educational dataset originally from a DataCamp project, published on Kaggle. Used for demonstration only.
